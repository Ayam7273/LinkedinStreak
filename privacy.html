Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

LinkedInStreak helps you keep a LinkedIn posting habit. This page explains what we collect, why, and the rights you have under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). We keep it short and in plain English.

Who we are

LinkedInStreak is run by Abdulbasit Adio, who is the "data controller" for your data. For anything about privacy or your data, contact:

  • Email: abdulbasitadio@gmail.com
  • LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/abdulbasit-adio-61376535b

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name Google shares with us.
  • Profile: your name, your public LinkedIn profile link, your timezone and which day your week starts.
  • Goals: the schedules you set, their changes over time and any pauses.
  • Posts you log: the post link, its ID, the publish time read from that ID, whether we could confirm you wrote it, and any note you add.
  • Notifications: if you turn on reminders, a push subscription for your browser (an address your browser gives us) and a log of reminders sent, so we never send the same one twice.
  • Security data: short-lived counters keyed to your account or IP address, used to stop abuse.

We never ask for your LinkedIn password and we don't connect to your LinkedIn account. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it (lawful basis)

  • To provide the service you signed up for: tracking your goals, verifying posts and sending reminders (performance of a contract).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse, such as two accounts claiming the same post (legitimate interests).

How we verify posts

We read the publish time from the ID inside the post link, compare the name in the link with your profile link, and may make one request to LinkedIn's public embed page for that post. We cache that public check for 24 hours. We don't store anything else from LinkedIn.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase stores the database and handles sign-in.
  • Vercel hosts the website and runs our server code.
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Your browser's push service (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla) delivers reminders.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. Push subscriptions that stop working are removed automatically. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, goals, posts, subscriptions and notification log straight away.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Two of these are built in: Settings → Export data downloads everything we hold about you as a file, and Settings → Delete account erases it. You can also object to or ask us to restrict processing. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app.
