How it works

Three steps. No LinkedIn password, no browser extension.

  1. Set your goal

    Pick the days you want to post, like Friday to Sunday, or a target such as three posts a week. Add an end date if you like.

  2. Post on LinkedIn

    Write and publish as you normally would. We'll remind you on posting days at times you choose.

  3. Paste the link, we verify it

    Copy the link to your post and paste it in, or share it straight from the LinkedIn app. We check the date and that it's yours.

Everything you need to stay consistent

Frequently Asked Questions

How does verification work?

Every LinkedIn post link contains an ID, and that ID encodes the moment the post was published. We read the date from it, check the post belongs to your profile using the name in the link, and make one public check of the post's embed page where we can. There's no LinkedIn API and no access to your account.

Do you need my LinkedIn password?

No. We never ask for your LinkedIn password and never connect to your LinkedIn account. You only give us your public profile link.

What if I post late at night?

Each goal has a grace period of up to 24 hours. The post must be published on the day, but you can log it the next morning.

Can I take a break?

Yes. Pause a goal for a holiday and the paused days never count against your streak.

Is it free?

Yes. You can export or delete your data at any time from Settings.
